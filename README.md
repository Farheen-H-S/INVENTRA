# INVENTRA
Inventra is a web-based Inventory Reconciliation &amp; Verification Service that helps  businesses compare their recorded inventory with their physical stock without requiring them to  replace their existing software.

Businesses can provide existing inventory and transaction information 
through software exports, spreadsheets, stock registers, or manual entry. During a physical stock check, the owner or employees can enter the actual quantities through a mobile-friendly interface 
using existing product IDs, SKUs, or simple product names with relevant variants.

The system compares the expected stock with the physical count and generates a reconciliation report 
highlighting differences that require attention. 

For example, if the expected quantity of a product is 
120 units but only 113 units are physically available, the system will identify the seven-unit variance 
for review. It will not automatically assume the reason for the discrepancy, since the cause may 
require additional information from the business.

The core service would be offered through a subscription, while optional paid human assistance
would be available for businesses that do not have sufficient time to conduct physical verification or 
need help entering handwritten records. Customers would remain responsible for providing the 
necessary information, while the service would reduce the effort required to organize, compare, and 
review their inventory.

The proposed customer segment, actual inventory-related difficulties, preferred service model, and 
willingness to pay will be validated through customer research with relevant businesses. The findings 
will be used to refine the target market, pricing, and service features.
